Project Objectives

Five structured objectives.

The project is structured around five clearly defined engineering objectives that address the design, evaluation, and comparative analysis of the RIS-aided dual antenna system.

01

Design RIS for obstacle detection in V2X environments.

02

Enhance signal reliability and coverage using RIS.

03

Detect and locate obstacles in NLOS environments.

04

Compare conventional and RIS-aided dual antenna systems.

05

Improve obstacle detection reliability using direct and RIS-assisted signal diversity.

Novelty

Key contributions.

Intelligent reflection: RIS intelligently reflects signals to extend coverage beyond line-of-sight, turning the physical environment into a controllable propagation medium.

Dual antenna configuration: The dual antenna design provides simultaneously available direct and RIS-reflected paths, creating spatial signal diversity not achievable with a single antenna.

LOS and NLOS detection: The system maintains obstacle detection performance in both line-of-sight and non-line-of-sight conditions — addressing the primary limitation of conventional sensing systems.

Enhanced reliability in dynamic environments: Signal diversity across two independent propagation paths provides robustness to the path-specific degradation that causes failure in single-path systems.
