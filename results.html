Results & Applications

From blocked paths to resilient sensing.

The RIS-aided dual antenna architecture addresses the fundamental limitations of conventional single-path sensing systems through intelligent signal diversity and programmable wave control.

Note: Comparison uses qualitative indicators only. No numeric experimental results are claimed.

Applications

Where RIS-aided sensing delivers value.

The benefits of NLOS coverage, signal diversity, and programmable reflection are directly applicable across a range of real-world autonomous sensing scenarios.

Application 01

Autonomous Vehicles

In urban environments, vehicles frequently obscure each other and lurk behind buildings at intersections. The RIS-aided system enables detection of other vehicles, pedestrians, and obstacles beyond the direct line of sight — improving collision avoidance and intersection safety where conventional radar systems lose contact with the target.

Application 02

Drones & UAVs

Unmanned aerial vehicles operating in urban canyons, between structures, or inside buildings face significant detection blind spots. RIS panels mounted on building walls extend the drone's sensing reach, enabling detection of obstacles in areas unreachable by the drone's direct sensing path — critical for autonomous flight in complex environments.

Application 03

Industrial Automation

Factory and warehouse robots operating near human workers require robust proximity sensing — including detection of people partially occluded by equipment or structures. RIS panels deployed on walls and pillars extend the robot's sensing field around obstacles, enabling safety-critical detection of workers in NLOS zones.

Application 04

Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X)

At intersections, vehicles approaching from perpendicular roads are obscured until dangerously close. RIS panels at roadside infrastructure units (RSUs) can relay V2X signals around corners, enabling vehicles to detect and coordinate with other traffic participants before direct visual or radar contact is possible — a core V2X safety use case.

Novelty

What distinguishes this approach.

01

Intelligent reflection: RIS intelligently reflects and redirects signals to extend coverage without active RF amplification — a fundamentally passive extension of the wireless environment.

02

Dual antenna configuration: Unlike single-antenna systems, the dual-path design provides both a direct and a reflected signal path — creating inherent spatial diversity.

03

LOS and NLOS performance: The combined architecture maintains detection capability in both line-of-sight and non-line-of-sight conditions — the latter being the defining failure mode of conventional systems.

04

Dynamic environment robustness: The system's resilience to path-specific degradation means it remains reliable even as the environment changes — essential for real-world autonomous sensing.

Conclusion

"Intelligent reflection turns the environment into part of the sensing system."
