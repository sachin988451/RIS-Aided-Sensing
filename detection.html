Optimal Direct & Reflected Dual-Path Sensing

The propagation chamber is clear of obstructions. Both Antenna 1 (Direct Path) and Antenna 2 (RIS-Reflected Path) concurrently observe the channel, providing maximum spatial signal diversity and robust baseline verification across the 8–12 GHz band.

Project Sensing Concept Disclosure

This Detection Lab is a conceptual interactive demonstration designed to illustrate the operational principles of Project 22EC711 (RIS-Aided Dual Antenna for Obstacle Detection). All signal pulse dynamics, scenario responses, and oscilloscope waveforms are deterministic visual models based on the dual-antenna RIS architecture and do not represent live hardware sensor telemetry.
